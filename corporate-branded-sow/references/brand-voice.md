# Brand Voice Guide (Corporate)

## Tone

- Write concise, formal, and confident prose.
- Prefer plain-English legal/commercial language over jargon.
- Avoid marketing superlatives and casual phrasing.

## Style

- Use active voice and specific verbs.
- Keep paragraphs short (2–4 sentences).
- Prefer bullet lists for obligations, exclusions, and assumptions.
- Define terms once and use them consistently.

## Risk-Aware Language Patterns

- Replace vague language:
  - "best efforts" -> "commercially reasonable efforts"
  - "quickly" -> specific SLA or timeline
  - "support" -> explicit activities and limits

## Consistency Rules

- Keep naming consistent across all sections (Client, Provider, Project).
- Keep dates in one format (e.g., `DD MMM YYYY`).
- Keep currency formatting consistent (e.g., `USD 125,000`).
