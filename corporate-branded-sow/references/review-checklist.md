# SOW Review Checklist

## Scope and Deliverables

- Scope boundaries are explicit and testable.
- Every deliverable has acceptance criteria and sign-off owner.
- Out-of-scope items prevent implied obligations.

## Commercial Integrity

- Fees, units, and totals reconcile.
- Invoice schedule aligns with milestones.
- Payment terms, taxes, and expense rules are unambiguous.

## Operational Feasibility

- Client responsibilities are explicit.
- Dependencies and assumptions are realistic.
- Timeline aligns with staffing and approval gates.

## Legal and Risk

- Data handling and confidentiality obligations are present.
- IP ownership and license grants are explicit.
- Limitation of liability and exclusions are not contradictory.

## Document Quality

- Section numbering is complete and stable.
- Defined terms are used consistently.
- No unresolved placeholders except in `Open Items / Client Decisions`.
