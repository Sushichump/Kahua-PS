# SOW Section Structure

Use this canonical order.

1. Executive Summary
   - Engagement purpose
   - Business outcomes

2. Scope of Services
   - In Scope
   - Out of Scope

3. Deliverables and Acceptance
   - Deliverable table
   - Acceptance method, timeline, and sign-off owner

4. Project Plan and Timeline
   - Milestones
   - Dependencies and client responsibilities

5. Roles and Governance
   - Team roles
   - Steering and escalation cadence

6. Commercial Terms
   - Pricing model
   - Fees and expenses
   - Invoicing and payment terms

7. Assumptions and Constraints

8. Change Control
   - Change request workflow
   - Impact analysis expectations

9. Legal and Compliance Provisions
   - Confidentiality and data handling
   - IP ownership and licensing
   - Warranty, liability, and exclusions

10. Term and Termination

11. Signatures
